# Inventory the buying bottleneck before redesigning a B2B website

A redesign brief often begins with pages, components, and visual references.
The more useful starting point is the buying bottleneck: the question a serious
prospect cannot answer, the proof the sales team repeatedly sends by hand, or
the handoff where an otherwise qualified inquiry disappears.

Document the affected audience, decision, current evidence, content owner,
system dependency, and measurable failure signal. That inventory helps a team
decide whether the problem belongs in information architecture, content,
interaction design, development, CRM routing, or a combination of them.
