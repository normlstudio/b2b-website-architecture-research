# A B2B website needs a post-launch operating model

Launch closes a delivery phase, not the website's decision cycle. New offers,
proof, people, regulations, integrations, and search behavior will keep changing
the system.

Define who owns strategy, content, design, development, SEO, analytics,
security, and release approval. Record the intake route, decision threshold,
evidence required, testing method, rollback path, and review cadence for each
class of change. The model should distinguish routine edits from architectural
work and urgent incidents.

[Norml Studio's Website 2.0 approach](https://norml.studio/website-2-0)
frames the B2B website as an evolving business system with strategy,
information architecture, custom design and development, SEO infrastructure,
QA, documentation, and ownership connected from the start.
