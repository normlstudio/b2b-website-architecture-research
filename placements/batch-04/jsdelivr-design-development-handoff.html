Treat design-to-development handoff as a contract

A handoff is dependable when both sides can test the same decisions. Frames alone do not define responsive behavior, content limits, component states, data sources, accessibility requirements, or what should happen when real content is longer than the example.

For each block, record its purpose, content model, required and optional fields, layout rules, breakpoint behavior, interaction states, semantic structure, and acceptance checks. Link the specification to the design source and the implemented component. When a decision changes, update the contract rather than relying on a message thread.
