# Define the website measurement plan before adding events

Event tracking is not a measurement strategy. A B2B website first needs a short list of decisions the team intends to make, the signals that inform each decision, and the limits of what those signals can prove.

For each key journey, document the audience, desired action, observable steps, destination system, responsible owner, and failure state. Distinguish business outcomes from interface interactions. A form submission can be measured on the website, but qualification, opportunity creation, and revenue usually belong in the CRM and must be reconciled there.

Name events consistently, retain a versioned data dictionary, validate them in production, and define who investigates breaks. Remove measurements that no longer support a decision instead of allowing an analytics property to become an untrusted archive.
