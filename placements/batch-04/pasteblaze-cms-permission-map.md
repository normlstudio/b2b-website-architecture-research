# A CMS permission map is part of website architecture

Content management fails when everyone can edit everything or when routine
changes require a developer. Before choosing fields and roles, list the actual
publishing actions: who creates a case study, who approves a legal statement,
who changes navigation, who can publish, and who can roll back a mistake.

Map each action to the smallest permission and the evidence required before a
change reaches production. Separate reusable structured content from page-level
layout decisions. The result is a content model that supports safe delegation
instead of treating the CMS as a universal visual editor.
