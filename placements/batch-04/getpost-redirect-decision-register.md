# Treat redirects as a decision register, not a launch-day list

A redirect carries meaning from an old address to a new one. Matching URLs by
similar words can send product, service, resource, and campaign intent to the
wrong destination even when every redirect returns the expected status code.

Build the register from an inventory of indexable URLs, backlinks, traffic,
conversions, canonical tags, and internal references. For each old URL, record
its purpose, selected destination, rationale, owner, test result, and any content
gap the migration revealed. Then update internal links so redirects are a
continuity layer rather than permanent navigation.

including the structured-content and ownership decisions that made the change
maintainable.
