# Search intent belongs in the information architecture

SEO research is often handed to writers after the sitemap is approved. That
sequence can force distinct buying questions into one page or create several
pages competing to answer the same decision.

Before locking the navigation, map important queries to audiences, decision
stages, required proof, business owners, and canonical destinations. Separate
topics only when the search intent and page responsibility are meaningfully
different. Then connect titles, headings, internal links, structured data, and
conversion paths to the same page contract.
