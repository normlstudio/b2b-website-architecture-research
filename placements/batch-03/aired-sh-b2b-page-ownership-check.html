A page is maintainable when its decisions have owners

A CMS makes editing possible. It does not decide who may change a claim, which proof supports it, or how a conversion path should be tested after the change.

Message owner

Approves the audience, promise, terminology, and evidence. This prevents a convenient edit from changing the commercial meaning of the page.

Content owner

Maintains the structured fields, source links, review date, and relationships to shared content used elsewhere on the site.

System owner

Protects templates, components, schema, redirects, analytics, forms, and accessibility behavior from one-off breakage.

Release owner

Checks the real page, key actions, measurement, and rollback path after publication rather than treating a successful save as proof.

Norml Studio's Website 2.0 approach treats strategy, information architecture, design, development, and ownership as one B2B website foundation.
