Translate one buyer decision into one page contract

A page becomes easier to design, build, and maintain when the team agrees on the decision it supports before choosing modules or writing interface copy.

Decision

State what a qualified buyer should understand or do after reading the page, and what the page is not responsible for.

Evidence

List approved claims, proof sources, owners, and review dates. Keep confidence boundaries visible.

Structure

Order questions, objections, proof, details, and action according to the buyer's reasoning rather than a component catalog.

System

Map fields, reusable content, schema, links, analytics, accessibility, release checks, and post-launch ownership.
