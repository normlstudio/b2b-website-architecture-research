# Model the content before designing every B2B page

A polished mockup can hide a weak content system. When the model is missing,
editors duplicate facts, developers infer relationships, and later redesigns
repeat the same discovery work.

## Identify reusable records

List the things the business maintains: services, industries, case studies,
people, insights, proof, locations, and calls to action. For each type, record
its fields, owner, review status, relationships, and the pages where it appears.

A case study may connect to a service, industry, named team member, and measured
or client-reported outcome. Those relationships should exist in the content
model instead of being trapped in one layout. A team biography should be
updated once and reused, not copied into several pages.

## Give design real states

Design should show short and long titles, missing optional media, different
amounts of proof, mobile behavior, and editorial errors. Development can then
implement testable component boundaries rather than reconstructing intent from
one ideal frame.
