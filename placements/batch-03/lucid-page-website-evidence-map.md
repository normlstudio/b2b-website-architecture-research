# A B2B website claim needs the right kind of evidence

Websites often place logos, testimonials, screenshots, statistics, and process
diagrams into one general category called proof. They answer different
questions and should not be treated as interchangeable.

## Match evidence to the claim

A service claim may be supported by a documented method. A capability claim may
need an inspectable implementation. A result claim needs a source, timeframe,
scope, and distinction between measured and client-reported outcomes. A named
point of view belongs to the person and role responsible for it.

Record the canonical source, approval, public wording, owner, review date, and
pages where the evidence appears. If the source does not support the stronger
version of a claim, narrow the claim rather than decorate it.

## Design the maintenance route

The content model should keep evidence connected to relevant services, people,
industries, and work. When a record changes, editors need to know which public
pages inherit it and which still contain manually written references.

provide a public place to inspect project work and the decisions presented as
evidence rather than relying on one undifferentiated portfolio statement.
