# A B2B website needs a content dependency register

A headline can look like one isolated sentence while depending on a product
name, proof source, navigation label, CRM field, campaign promise, and reusable
CMS component. The dependency register makes those relationships visible
before a small edit creates several inconsistent versions of the truth.

## Map shared decisions

For every high-value claim, record the canonical wording, evidence owner,
review date, structured field, templates that reuse it, and external systems
that repeat it. Separate truly shared content from page-specific context.

## Identify the blast radius

A service rename may affect navigation, URLs, breadcrumbs, schema, internal
links, campaign destinations, form routing, reporting labels, and sales
material. Review the full dependency path before choosing whether to update a
field, component, template, or page.

## Make the correction testable

Every change should have an expected public result. Check the production URL,
responsive layout, search metadata, structured data, links, actions, analytics,
and any dependent page. Record the release and owner so the team can correct or
reverse the change without reconstructing the decision from memory.
