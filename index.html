Review the website as an operating system.

A visual refresh can leave the original structural problems intact. The method follows the decisions that determine whether the site can explain the company, support sales, rank, and keep changing without another rebuild.

  1. 1

    Define the decision job

    Record the buyers, questions, objections, proof, and actions the website must support before judging its pages.

  2. 2

    Trace the information architecture

    Map pages, navigation, content relationships, reusable templates, and the routes a buyer follows to understand the offer.

  3. 3

    Verify the operating layer

    Inspect CMS ownership, component reuse, source control, SEO foundations, measurement, accessibility, QA, and handover.

A review another team member can inspect.

The worksheet keeps the tested URL, review date, stakeholder job, architecture observation, evidence, owner, and next verification together. It separates what is visible from what requires access to code, CMS, analytics, or internal documentation.

Use it before a redesign, during discovery, or as a recheck after launch. The goal is not one universal score. It is a defensible record of which website decisions are stable, missing, duplicated, or trapped in one person’s head.

A website system should outlive the launch.

The architecture is not only a sitemap. It includes the content model, reusable page patterns, component states, ownership boundaries, source control, analytics, QA, documentation, and the process for changing the site safely.

A useful review asks whether the team can publish a new page, update a claim, extend a template, diagnose a regression, and understand why a decision was made without recreating the project from old messages.
